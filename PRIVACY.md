# Privacy

The switcher stores account labels, colors, timestamps, and hashed account fingerprints locally. Authentication snapshots and rollback backups are encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI. It does not send telemetry, analytics, credentials, or profile metadata to Vaguul or any third party.

Opening and using Codex remains subject to Codex's own network behavior and policies. Deleting a non-active profile removes its encrypted vault snapshot; up to five encrypted rollback backups may remain until newer successful switches prune them.
